Frequently Asked Questions

Is Fambly safe for kids?

Yes. Kids can only spend what you approve, and every card can be frozen from your lock screen.

Can I move from a jam jar?

Tap Import, type how much is in the jar, and Fambly starts counting from there. Keep the jar for sentimental reasons.

What age is Fambly for?

Six to sixteen. Younger kids get stickers, older kids get a debit card and a monthly budget.

Does it work on every phone?

Parents on older phones can pay and approve from the web app today; the full app ships in spring.
